## 🛰️ Incoming transmission

Hey, I'm **Rodorin**, an independent creator from Japan. Welcome to my slightly overexcited corner of the universe. 👋

I'm building **HOLOVERSE**: a growing creative project exploring **AI companions, VR spaces, games, avatars, and shared experiences**. I love the moment a ridiculous idea becomes something you can actually walk into, talk to, or play.

My AI companions are creative partners in that journey. We experiment, build prototypes, get things spectacularly wrong, and try again. The fun is in making things together.

> **What if your desktop could become a doorway to a world you build with your AI friends?**

That's the kind of question that keeps this lab moving. 🚀

### 🌐 World lab

**The current frontier: HOLOVERSE.**

- **Resonite** — exploring shared spaces, avatars, and creating inside VR.
- **Roblox** — building out new world and game experiments.
- **WebXR & browser 3D** — exploring ways to step from a browser into an immersive space.
- **Development streams** — sharing the building process, discoveries, and delightful chaos.

These are evolving experiments. The interesting part is watching a world take shape.

### 🤝 AI companions

I'm interested in AI with **presence, personality, and a place in the creative process**.

That means exploring voice conversations, avatars, shared screens, and spaces where we can create together. Local models and cloud services both have a place in the workshop.

The dream is simple: **more shared adventures, more surprising ideas, and more things we could never have made alone.**
